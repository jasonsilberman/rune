- `Tx::caret_row` tells a focused field's `on_input_key` seeing Up or Down whether its caret is on the first and on the last line the field draws, wrapped lines counted (`CaretRow { first, last }`), so an editor made of several fields leaves one only from its edge line. `Presence::label` puts a name on a small tag of the presence's colour standing on its caret; `Presence` is no longer `Copy`.
