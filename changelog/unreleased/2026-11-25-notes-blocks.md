- Rich text fields gain notes blocks: `Block::Heading(1..=3)` draws a title, heading or subheading larger and bolder with room above it, and `Block::Check(bool)` draws a checklist item with a round checkbox that a press in its gutter toggles (`TextEdit::toggle_check`, one undo step, firing the field's change handler), its checked text struck through and dimmed. `TextEdit::markdown_shortcuts` turns `# `, `## `, `### `, `- `, `1. `, `> `, `[] `, `- [ ] ` and `[x] ` at a line's start into blocks. `El::presence` with `Presence::new(start, end, color)` tints other people's selections and draws their carets in a shared field. Copied HTML carries `<h1>`–`<h3>` and checkbox list items.
