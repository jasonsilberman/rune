- Diff word changes are tinted 0.28 of the way from the background to the side's hue in light and 0.26 in dark (from 0.27 and 0.32), which keeps syntax colours at about 3.5:1 or better on them, as GitHub's own word backgrounds do. `InlineDiff::font` draws code and line numbers in a given style, each row as tall as its line height. `InlineDiff::marked` lines keep an added or removed line's tint and take the selected tint in the gutter and the selected ink on their numbers.
