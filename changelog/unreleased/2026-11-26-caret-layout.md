- `Cx::caret_layout(key, offset)` reads where a caret at a UTF-16 offset of a text element sits after layout, `[x, y, w, h]` unscrolled like `Cx::layout`, whether or not anything is focused there: a `text`'s content, or a `text_input`'s edit text mapped to what it displays (past a list item's marker). A view places a collaborator's name flag beside their caret with it, and re-runs when that caret moves.
